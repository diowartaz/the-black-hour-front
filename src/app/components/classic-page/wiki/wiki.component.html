Overview

You are the last human alive in a ruined city.

Each night, waves of zombiesattack your defenses.
Each day, you dig, build, and learnin an effort to survive one more night.
When your city falls, you earn coins to buy new building plans and permanent bonuses for your next games.
Upgrade your plans, trade them on the Black Market, and join a guild to earn even more.
Are you ready to climb the ranks and dominate the leaderboard?

A day in the city

You wake up at 8:00 and the day ends at midnight. An action that would end after midnight can't be started.
Time flows for real: a full day lasts 5 real minutes. The time you spend thinking between two actions is added to the next one, so don't wait too long!
The three tabs at the bottom of the screen are your actions:
Action What it does
Dig Find resources. A dig takes 2 hours (less with skills and bonuses).
Learn Learn a skill level you have unlocked.
Build Spend resources to build or upgrade a building.

The city header

Indicator Meaning
Day The current day of your city.
Time The current time in your city.
Def Your defense. A +min–max next to it is extra defense that only counts for tonight's attack.
Threat How many zombies will attack tonight (min–max). It turns red when the attack could break through your defense.

Sleeping

Can't fit another action before midnight?
End the day with this button: Sleep
Half of the time left before midnight is given back the next morning: you wake up earlier, by up to 3 hours. For example, sleeping at 10pm (2h left) wakes you up 1h earlier; sleeping at 6pm or earlier gives the full 3h.
Some special buildings improve your sleep.

Defense and attacks

Your city starts with 20 defense. Buildings add more.
The horde grows every night: it is the previous night's horde, plus a random number of zombies between day × 2 and day × 4. From day 15, this growth increases by another 10% per day.
≥ → You survive (a tie is enough).
< → Your city falls and the game ends.
After each attack, a recap shows your defense against the zombies and the margin you had.

Digging

There are 5 resources: wood, metal, stone, screw and patch. Buildings need them.
Pick: each dig offers two resources. Choose the one you keep.
Random: dig once, or as many times as you can before midnight (Max). The first dig gives one of the two offered resources at random. The next ones give any resource, each with the same probability.
A new pair of resources is offered after every dig action.
With the Lucky Digger bonus, a dig can find one more resource. On the first dig, you get both offered resources.

Buildings

Each building plan has a cost in resources, a build time and a number of levels. Every level you build adds its defense to your city.

Special buildings have no fixed defense but have an effect: hover or tap them to read it. Any defense they give benefits from your Fortifier bonus, limits included, and the effect shown during a game already counts it. What they did shows up in a pop-up after your action, and in the Night log of the attack recap.

Discovering plans

You start with 7 plans drawn from your deck.
Every city has a Blueprint Workshop. After each night survived, each of its levels has a 70% chance to discover a plan. If it discovers N plans, you choose up to N among N + 2 plans drawn from your deck.
When 15 buildings of your deck (75%) reach their max level, every completed building goes back into the draw pile. Picking one again unlocks a new set of levels for it.

Skills

Each skill improves a specific aspect of survival. Effects are linear per level.

Skill Effect per level Max level 1st level
Digger Digging takes 10% less time. 5 (time ÷ 2) 6h
Builder Building takes 10% less time. 5 (time ÷ 2) 3h
Fast Learner Learning takes 10% less time. 5 (time ÷ 2) 2h
Insomniac Wake up 15 min earlier. 8 (2h earlier) 3h
Each level takes 1.5 times longer to learn than the previous one.
A skill level must be unlocked before you can learn it. You start with 9 levels unlocked at random.
Every city has Archives. After each night survived, each of its levels has a 50% chance to unlock a random skill level (no choice).
Some special buildings raise the max level of skills.

Deck and collection

Your collection holds every building plan you own. Your deck is made of exactly 20 different plans, with no limit on rarity: your starting plans and your Blueprint Workshop discoveries are drawn from it.

Plans are common, rare or epic. You start with 20 plans (14 common and 6 rare).
Autocomplete fills your deck with your rarest plans while keeping resources balanced. The Resources needed bar shows what your deck will ask for: digs give each resource 20% of the time.
Your deck is locked in when you create a city: changes apply to your next city.
A deck only uses one copy of a plan: upgrade it with your duplicates, or sell them on the Black Market. The choice is yours.

Plan ranks

Each plan starts at rank 0 and goes up to rank 10. Every rank adds 20% of the base value to the building's defense and effect, effect limits included, up to +200% at rank 10. Resource costs, build times and levels do not change.

Open a plan with the Upgrade button of your collection. Nothing upgrades on its own, and an upgrade cannot be undone: the copies are used for good, and you keep your card.

Black Market

The Black Market opens after 5 finished games. Players trade plans there, one copy at a time or in lots of 10, without knowing who they deal with.

Buying: only the cheapest offer of each plan is shown, one for single copies and one for lots of 10. When it is bought, the next one takes its place. You always pay the price you see: if someone else was faster, nothing is charged.
Selling: you always keep one copy of a plan: selling one needs 2 copies, a lot of 10 needs 11. You set the price of the whole lot and pay a 5% tax on it when listing. The tax is never refunded.
The plans leave your collection as soon as they are listed. You get the coins when it sells, even if you are offline, and you can withdraw an unsold offer at any time.

Rewards and XP

You start with 1 000 coins.
When your city falls, each night survived pays: the 1st night 34 coins, the 2nd 38, the 3rd 42… (30 + 4 per night). That's 520 coins for 10 nights, 1 440 for 20 and 2 760 for 30.
Reward Amount
Base Coins for the nights survived.
Ranked +25% of the base, in ranked games.
Victory +40% of all your coins from the game, if you win a ranked duel.
Money Maker +3% per Money Maker level.
In a guild, your coins also feed the guild pot, paid to its members every day.
Every coin earned in a game is also earned as XP. Your XP raises your level, shown on your profile.

Ranked mode

Don't hesitate to play ranked to earn more (+25%, +75% if you win)!

A ranked game is a duel: two players face the exact same zombie waves, one after the other, and the best survivor wins ranked points.

Finding an opponent

When you start a ranked game, you join a game whose first player has already fallen. You won't see their score until you fall too.
If no game is waiting, you play first: your result waits for an opponent to take up the challenge.

The same zombie waves for both players

Both players share the same random draws: same zombie waves night after night, same starting skills, same resources offered dig after dig. Each player brings their own deck and plan ranks: building them well is part of the duel.
Your permanent bonuses still apply in ranked.

Who wins?

Order Criterion
1 The player who survives the most days wins.
2 Same day: the highest defense on the night of the fall wins.
3 Same day and same defense: it's a draw, nobody wins or loses points.

Ranked points

Every player starts with 500 points. Points are computed like chess Elo: what the winner earns, the loser loses (always less than 48 per game). Beating a stronger player earns more, losing to them costs less.
Your opponent has… If you win If you lose
the same points as you +24 -24
200 points more +36 -12
400 points more +44 -4
Points are given once both players have fallen. Until then, the game shows as pending in your match history, then as Victory, Defeat or Draw.

Ranked coins

When your city falls, you earn 25% more coins, win or lose. They are credited right away, without waiting for your opponent.
The winner of the duel earns 40% more on top of all their coins from the game: 75% more than a normal game. If you played second, you get it as soon as your city falls; if you played first, it is credited when your opponent falls or runs out of time.

Time limit

You have 4 real hours from the moment you enter a ranked game to finish it. A countdown is shown above your city and turns red in the last 30 minutes. Your city is never lost: past this delay, it goes on as a normal game, without ranked coins.
If you joined as the second player, you lose by forfeit, with your city's current day and defense. If you play first, the ranked game is cancelled, without ranked points.

Leaderboards and profile

Leaderboard Ranking
Ranked By ranked points. You appear after your first completed ranked game.
Normal By best day reached. Players on the same day are ranked by the biggest attack they survived.
Tap a player to open their profile: level, XP, guild, rank, ranked points, records and their last 20 games, with each ranked duel's opponent, result and victory bonus.
Your own profile also shows what you earned today and your share of the guild pot.

Guilds

A guild gathers up to 20 players who share part of their earnings. Open it from the Guild menu. You can only be in one guild at a time.

The guild pot

Every game a member finishes adds 1% of its coins (ranked victory bonus included) to the day's pot. It takes nothing from their own coins.
After midnight (Paris time), every member who finished at least one game that day receives the whole pot. The more members play, the bigger it gets for everyone.
Your share follows you if you change guild. If you have no guild at midnight, it is lost.

Joining and leading

Public guilds with free places can be joined directly. They are listed with the biggest pot of the day first, and you can search them by name.
Private guilds are joined on invitation only. An invitation lasts 7 days.
Anyone without a guild can create one and becomes its leader. The name (3 to 24 characters) can't be changed later. The leader chooses whether the guild is public or private, invites players who have no guild, and can kick members.
Members can leave at any time. If the leader leaves, the guild is disbanded.

Invite friends

Share your invite code or link (menu Invite friends). A friend who signs up with it becomes your referee:
You earn 10% of the coins they earn at the end of each of their games, forever. It takes nothing from their own coins.
They get a welcome gift of 2 500 coins.
You can have up to 3 referees, and remove one to free a spot. A sponsor's account must be older than yours, and your sponsor can't be changed once set.

Account and settings

You can play as a guest, without an account. Your progress is not saved online: open Settings and use Save my account to keep it.
Pick your theme in Settings: Neon Arcade, Quarantine, Hemorrhage or Signal.
Pick your language in Settings: English or French. The game starts in French if your browser is set to French.
You can also log out or delete your account there.

Strategy tips

Upgrade the Blueprint Workshop and the Archives early: more plans and more skills for the rest of the game.
Keep an eye on the Threat: when it turns red, build defense before anything else.
Don't waste the end of the day: if no action fits before midnight, sleep to wake up earlier.
Pick the resources your next buildings need, and keep a deck with balanced resource needs.
Play ranked for 25% more coins (75% if you win), and buy Money Maker early to earn more from every game.
Use your duplicates to upgrade the plans you build the most, and sell the rest on the Black Market.
Join an active guild and finish at least one game a day to get the pot.
